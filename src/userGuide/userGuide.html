User Guide

Quick Popup Features

Access the quick popup to easily manage your timer. Here’s what you can do:

  • Start Timer: Quickly begin your Pomodoro timer.
  • Pause Timer: Pause the timer whenever needed.
  • Skip Timer: Skip to the next session or break.
  • Stop Timer: End the current session entirely.
  • Quick Links: Quick links to useful pages.

Main Page

Click on the clock area in the popup to go to the main page.
The main page provides:

  • Quick options to control your timer.
  • Short inspiring quotes for your sessions or breaks.
  • Music player to listen to some soothing music.
  • Navigation links to other pages like
    Settings, History, or Help/Support.
    (At the top of the timer)

History Page

Track your progress and review your past sessions with:

  • Detailed visualizations of your productivity data.
  • Graphs showing trends over time.
  • Insights into how you’re utilizing your focus sessions.

Settings Page

Personalize your experience with various customization options, such as:

  • Adjusting timer durations for focus sessions and breaks.
  • Enabling or disabling sound notifications.
  • Choosing themes or appearance settings.
